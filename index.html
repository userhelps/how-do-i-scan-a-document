How Do I Scan a Document? - Document Scanning Guide

Need to scan a document? You can scan paperwork using a multifunction printer, dedicated scanner, Windows computer, Mac, Android phone, or iPhone. This guide explains simple ways to scan documents and save them as PDF or image files.

What Do I Need to Scan a Document?

  • A document you want to scan.
  • A printer with scanning capability or a dedicated scanner.
  • A computer or mobile device, depending on your scanning method.
  • A USB or WiFi connection if required by your scanner.
  • Enough storage space to save the scanned document.

How Do I Scan a Document With a Printer?

Using a Printer Scanner:

  1. Turn on your printer or multifunction device.
  2. Lift the scanner lid or open the document feeder.
  3. Place the document face down on the scanner glass, or place it in the document feeder.
  4. Align the document with the scanner guides.
  5. Open the printer's scanning software on your computer or mobile device.
  6. Select the connected scanner or printer.
  7. Choose a scan type such as document, color, or black and white.
  8. Select PDF or another desired file format.
  9. Start the scan.
  10. Save the scanned file to your preferred location.

How Do I Scan a Document on Windows?

Windows Scanning:

  1. Connect your scanner or multifunction printer to your Windows computer.
  2. Make sure the scanner is powered on.
  3. Open the Windows scanning application or your scanner manufacturer's software.
  4. Select your scanner.
  5. Place the document on the scanner glass or in the document feeder.
  6. Choose your preferred scan settings.
  7. Start the scan.
  8. Save the scanned document.

How Do I Scan a Document on a Mac?

Mac Scanning:

  1. Connect your scanner or multifunction printer to your Mac.
  2. Turn on the scanner.
  3. Open your Mac's printer and scanner settings.
  4. Select the connected scanner.
  5. Place your document on the scanner glass or in the document feeder.
  6. Choose your scan settings.
  7. Start the scan.
  8. Select where you want to save the scanned file.

How Do I Scan a Document With Android?

Many Android phones can scan documents using a compatible camera or document scanning feature. You can use an appropriate scanning application to capture the document and save it as a PDF or image.

General Android Scanning:

  1. Open a document-scanning or camera application.
  2. Place the document on a flat, well-lit surface.
  3. Position the camera so the entire document is visible.
  4. Capture the document.
  5. Adjust the edges if the application provides automatic document detection.
  6. Review the scanned page.
  7. Add additional pages if necessary.
  8. Save or export the document as a PDF or image.

How Do I Scan a Document on iPhone?

Using iPhone Document Scanning:

  1. Open a compatible document-scanning feature or application.
  2. Place the document on a flat surface with good lighting.
  3. Position your iPhone above the document.
  4. Capture the document when the scanning feature detects it.
  5. Adjust the document edges if necessary.
  6. Scan additional pages if needed.
  7. Save the completed scan as a PDF or image.

How Do I Scan Multiple Pages Into One PDF?

Multi-Page Scanning:

  1. Start a document scan using your scanner or scanning application.
  2. Scan the first page.
  3. Select the option to add another page.
  4. Continue scanning each page in the correct order.
  5. Review the pages before saving.
  6. Choose PDF as the output format.
  7. Save the completed multi-page document.

How Do I Scan a Document to PDF?

PDF is a convenient format when you need to send or store a document. When configuring your scan, select PDF as the output format if your scanner or scanning application provides that option.

  • Use a suitable resolution for readable text.
  • Choose color or black-and-white scanning based on the document.
  • Preview the scan before saving.
  • Check that all pages are correctly oriented.
  • Use a descriptive filename so the document is easy to find later.

What If My Scanner Is Not Detected?

Try these steps:
  • Make sure the scanner or printer is powered on.
  • Check the USB or WiFi connection.
  • Restart the scanner and your computer or phone.
  • Make sure the device is connected to the same WiFi network when required.
  • Check for available scanner or printer software updates.
  • Verify that the correct scanner is selected in your scanning application.

How Do I Get Better Scan Quality?

  • Clean the scanner glass before scanning.
  • Place the document straight and flat.
  • Use adequate lighting when scanning with a phone.
  • Choose an appropriate resolution for the document.
  • Preview each page before saving.
  • Avoid shadows, glare, and reflections.

Final Advice

The easiest way to scan a document depends on the equipment you have. A multifunction printer can scan directly to a computer or mobile device, while modern smartphones can capture documents using scanning features. For important documents, review every page carefully before saving or sharing the final PDF.
